Section 1 // Institutional Specification

1. Executive Abstract & Core Philosophy

The Adaptive AI Trading Decision-Support System is an institutional-grade financial intelligence engine engineered to assist quantitative analysts, portfolio managers, and risk committees in capital allocation and trade selection.

Traditional algorithmic tools suffer from two catastrophic failure modes:

  • Black-box statistical/deep learning models that overfit historical regimes and fail unpredictably during non-stationary regime shifts.
  • Naive LLM-based stock predictors that hallucinate arithmetic, confuse sentiment with trading signals, and lack risk governance.
The Core Architectural Axiom: Strictly separate deterministic quantitative mathematics from structured, context-aware AI reasoning. Numerical calculations (indicators, volatility, covariance, drawdowns, slippage) are executed exclusively by deterministic Python/NumPy modules. Large Language Models and ensemble agents are utilized strictly for higher-order reasoning.

3. Deterministic Quantitative Indicators

All numerical computations run strictly outside the LLM in CPython and NumPy:

Realized Annualized Volatility

Standard 20-day and 60-day rolling log-return deviation:

$$\sigma_{\text{ann}} = \sqrt{252} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{1}{N-1} \sum_{i=1}^N (r_i - \bar{r})^2}$$

Parkinson High-Low Volatility

Extreme-value volatility estimator capturing intraday range:

$$\sigma_P = \sqrt{\frac{252}{4 \ln(2) \cdot N} \sum_{i=1}^N \ln\left(\frac{H_i}{L_i}\right)^2}$$

Downside Semi-Deviation & Sortino

Penalizes exclusively downside returns below target:

$$\sigma_{\text{down}} = \sqrt{\frac{252}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \min(0, r_i - r_f)^2}$$

Relative Volume (RVOL)

Current volume normalized against 20-day moving average:

$$\text{RVOL}_{20} = \frac{V_t}{\text{SMA}_{20}(V)}$$

5. Multi-Horizon Reasoning & Tension Analysis

Every trade proposal is simultaneously evaluated across three independent timeframes:

Short-Term (1–5 Days)

Intraday/daily momentum, oversold oscillator rebounds, RVOL surges, and immediate breaking news catalysts.

Medium-Term (2–8 Weeks)

Intermediate moving averages, sector rotation trends, macroeconomic regime state, and earnings revisions.

Long-Term (3–12 Months)

200 EMA secular structure, fundamental valuation multiples, competitive moat, and persistent industry trends.

Horizon Tension Detection: When tactical signals conflict with secular trends (e.g. Short-Term BUY inside Long-Term BEAR), the system automatically restricts position sizes and tightens trailing stop-loss triggers.

7. Model Council & Shannon Entropy Disagreement

Five specialized models independently cast conviction-weighted votes:

  • Technical Trend Model: Multi-moving average structure and momentum.
  • Statistical Mean-Reversion Model: Bollinger z-score dispersion and RSI extremes.
  • Quantitative ML Model: Random Forest classifier on tabular feature matrices.
  • Regime Specialist Model: Macro regime alignment and transition probability.
  • News & Event Specialist: Materiality-weighted sentiment extraction.
Normalized Shannon Entropy Formula:
$$H(V) = -\sum_{i=1}^K p(a_i) \log_K p(a_i)$$

When entropy exceeds 0.50, decision confidence is sharply penalized, and the system considers abstaining.

8. Calibrated Uncertainty & First-Class Abstention

Confidence is an empirical calculation, never an LLM hallucination:

$$\text{Confidence} = \phi(\text{Signal Strength}) \times (1 - \text{Disagreement}) \times \text{Regime Stability} \times \text{Data Integrity}$$
$$\text{Composite Uncertainty} = 1.0 - \text{Calibrated Confidence}$$
NO_TRADE as a First-Class Decision: The platform explicitly abstains when calibrated confidence is low (< 45%), model disagreement is high (> 0.50), the regime is CRISIS_STRESS, or estimated transaction friction exceeds expected alpha.

10. Counterfactual Scenarios ("What Would Change My Mind?")

Every proposed trade generates three forward stress scenarios and explicit thesis invalidators:

  • Base Case: Expected price drift, target price, and probability.
  • Adverse Case: Moderate correction scenario with stop-out levels.
  • Severe Shock: Tail-risk shock evaluating drawdown under extreme market stress.

11. Contextual Decision Memory & Self-Correction

Separates Decision Quality from Outcome Luck using a 2×2 attribution grid:

GOOD_DECISION_GOOD_OUTCOME

Sound multi-factor process; statistical edge realized as expected.

GOOD_DECISION_BAD_OUTCOME

Disciplined process; outcome attributable to exogenous market variance.

BAD_DECISION_GOOD_OUTCOME

Flawed process/high conflict trade that got lucky. Penalized in memory.

BAD_DECISION_BAD_OUTCOME

Predictable failure due to ignored risk constraints or regime mismatch.
